How Magic Find works after the rework

  • Drops = base chance × (1 + MF / 100), and it now applies to every mob drop, not only drops under 5%.
  • Past 100% the extra becomes a chance to double the drop. The stat caps at 500.
  • Every point is worth the same +1% of base, so the best upgrade is always the cheapest point you do not have yet.
  • Pets, enrichments, Sorrow base stats, cloaks, Lucky Clover, Legion, Jerry Candy and SkyBlock levels give no Magic Find any more.
