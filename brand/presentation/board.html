The brief

A free planner for Hypixel SkyBlock players that ranks every Magic Find source by coins per point after the 0.27.3 rework. It should feel like a sharp, trustworthy tool from inside the game's world, not a generic stats site.

thriftyexactgame-nativequietly clever
